# Strategy — "Have You Tried Turning It Off?"

## The entry

> Okay, deep breath: unplug the router, count to 47, plug it back in whispering 'you're doing amazing, sweetie.' It just needs to feel appreciated.

## Why this line wins (judge criteria, in order)

1. **A funny single-line reply from a hopeless tech support agent to a desperate
   customer.** The speaker is unmistakably a support agent mid-call, and the
   customer is mid-panic — the opening "Okay, deep breath:" is the agent
   calming someone down, not fixing anything.
2. **Lands the joke.** The twist: the classic "turn it off and on again" is
   replaced by a superstitious ritual — count to a specific odd number, whisper
   praise to the hardware. The humour comes entirely from the agent's cheerful
   uselessness ("It just needs to feel appreciated"), and the customer is never
   the butt of the joke; the agent is on the customer's side, just no help at
   all.
3. **Sounds like a real agent line.** "Okay, deep breath:" is exactly how a
   real agent talks to a panicking caller; the customer's desperation is felt
   in that opening beat, never explained.
4. **Tight.** One line, one ritual, one punchline. Every word earns its place;
   the joke is clear on a first read.
5. **Fresh, with a twist on the classic.** It leans on the "turn it off and on
   again" classic and twists it into a therapy session for the router.

## The one specific detail no other entry has

The number **47** plus the whispered pet name **"sweetie"** — a concrete,
odd, memorable ritual (count to 47, whisper praise) that no generic
"restart your router" line can claim.

## Candidates tried and dropped

- "Unplug the router, count to 47 out loud, then whisper 'you've got this'
  before you plug it back in — it responds to encouragement." — Dropped: no
  customer desperation felt; reads like an instruction manual, not a call.
- "Let's give it a little spa day: unplug the router, count to 47, and plug it
  back in while whispering 'you've got this, champ.'" — Dropped: "spa day" is
  a second joke competing with the ritual; weaker punchline.
- "I hear you — let's unplug the router, count slowly to 47, and whisper 'we
  believe in you' while it rests. Routers can tell when we're stressed." —
  Dropped: explains the joke ("routers can tell...") instead of landing it;
  too long.
- "Have you tried turning it off, counting to 47, and whispering 'you're doing
  great, little guy' while it boots back up?" — Dropped: a question, not an
  agent's instruction; quotes the classic instead of twisting it.

## Expected outcome

A short, specific, agent-voiced line with a clear twist and a punchline should
beat generic restart lines in pairwise judging. Ties break on smaller text and
earlier receipt, so submitting v1 early matters. If the live score comes back
low, the judge's hidden reasons decide the next version — never change the
"47 + whisper" detail, which is the entry's identity.
